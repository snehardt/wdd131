Overview

Purpose

The purpose of this website is to showcase the different classes that you can build your character as, and the different playstyles you can play as. It will help players to understand how to play each class, and ideas for different challenges. It will be easier to choose which playstyle they want to play.

Audience

The intended audience is for gamers, specifically the ones that play Terraria who want to try to improve, or play a different playstyle. It will also be for new players starting the game, to show the main builds they can play.

Dynamic elements

JavaScript will be used to create interactive elements on the site. Users will be able to click on images to display more information about each build. Buttons will allow users to show or hide detailed descriptions of builds.

Content

Home page

Terraria is a game with a lot of different ways to play depending on the class and playstyle you choose. This site goes over the main builds in the game, including melee, mage, ranger, and summoner.

If you're new or just want to try something different, this site can help you find a build that fits how you like to play.

Melee: Melee is a class that mainly uses swords to fight the enemies. It has the most defense out of all the classes, with the least amount of range. There are a lot of accessories to help you build the character you want.

Mage: Mage class uses mana to shoot spells, which do a lot of damage. With the amount of damage they do, they don't have a lot of defense, which makes them a glass cannon.

Ranger: The ranger class uses ammo to shoot at the enemy with a lot of range. Keeping your distance is a great idea for rangers, who have defense, but are still pretty fragile.

Summoner: Summoners rely on minions to deal most of their damage, while using whips to control enemies. They can be weaker on their own, but their minions make up for it.

Builds

Each class in Terraria plays differently. Melee focuses on close combat and defense, mage uses strong magic attacks, ranger uses bows and guns, and summoner lets minions fight for you. The best class really depends on how you like to play.

Each build includes recommended weapons and armor to help you get started. You can click on each one to learn more about how it works.

Pre-hard Mode: In Terraria, there are two modes that you play in the same game. There is pre-hard mode, and then hard mode. Pre-hard mode has easier enemies and easier bosses to fight. There are certain items that you can get in pre-hard mode, which are the best for this part of the game. It is the Obsidian armor, Jungle armor, Necro armor, and Molten armor.

Molten Armor: Molten armor is crafted by obtaining hellstone from the underworld. You need a meteorite pickaxe to be able to mine it. It is the best armor for melee class as it gives you a bonus of extra melee damage. It also grants a 25 defense, which is very strong in pre-hard mode.

Jungle Armor: Jungle armor is the best armor for mages, which is crafted by vines, jungle spores, and stingers. It grants a defense of 17 with a bonus of 80 extra mana and a reduced mana cost. It is great for dealing a lot of damage.

Necro Armor: Necro Armor is best for the ranger class. It is crafted by cobwebs and bones. It grants a defense of 19 with a bonus of 10% increase of critical strike chance for ranged weapons. You can only obtain it after defeating Skeletron, guarding the dungeon.

Obsidian Armor: Obsidian Armor is the best for summoner class, which has the least amount of defense, and allows your summons to deal more damage. It is crafted by using silk, obsidian, and tissue samples/shadow scales. It has a defense of 15, with a bonus of whip range increase along with speed. It also increases summon damage by 15%.

Hard Mode: You enter Hard mode by defeating the Wall of Flesh. Your world changes, creating new biomes and new ores for you to obtain. Using all of these new ores, you can craft better weapons and better armor. Enemies become a lot stronger, so if you don't level up fast, you'll have a hard time fighting enemies that you once could defeat easily.

Solar Flare: Solar flare armor the best for the melee class in Hard mode. It is end game armor, after defeating the Moon Lord. It is crafted with solar fragments and luminite bars. This armor set allows the player to dash, hurting enemies in front of them. This armor grant 78 defense as well, making it the highest defense in the game.

Nebula Armor: Nebula armor is the best armor to use for the mage class, which is also end game armor. You can craft it by using nebula fragments and luminite bars. It grants the player 46 defense, making it a good damage armor, but not as survivable. The bonus grants attacks to spawn buffs, that grant extra damage.

Vortex Armor: Vortex armor is the best Hard mode armor set for rangers. It is crafted in the same way that all of the other armor sets are, with vortex fragments and luminite bars. It grants the player 62 defense and has a stealth mode, slowing movement, but making enemies less likely to target you.

Stardust Armor: Stardust armor is the best summoner armor, crafted with stardust fragments and luminite bars. The stardust armor has a defense of 38 and spawns an extra summon, the stardust guardian, that will stay close and protect you from enemies. It also allows for 9 additional summons that will deal a lot of damage.

Playstyles

Playstyles in Terraria can completely change how the game feels, even if you use the same class. Some players like to play aggressively and deal as much damage as possible, while others focus more on defense and staying alive.

You can also make your own challenges, like limiting what weapons you use or trying to beat the game without taking damage. These kinds of playstyles make the game more interesting and give you new ways to play.

Aggressive Playstyle: An aggressive playstyle is all about dealing damage quickly and staying on the attack. Players using this style focus on strong weapons, fast movement, and taking down enemies before they become a threat. This approach works well for experienced players who are confident in dodging and positioning during fights.

Defensive Playstyle: A defensive playstyle focuses on staying alive and reducing damage as much as possible. Players using this style often choose stronger armor, healing items, and safer positioning in battles. It may be slower, but it can make difficult fights much more manageable, especially for newer players.

Solo vs Multiplayer: Playing alone and playing with others can feel very different. Solo players need to balance damage, defense, and survival on their own, while multiplayer allows players to take on different roles. For example, one player can focus on damage while another supports or distracts enemies. Solo can be challenging, but also very rewarding for soloing all of the bosses. Multiplayer can also be very fun, making it easier to find the ores and weapons that are useful and strong.

Challenges Playstyle: After beating the game one time, and getting the hang of all of the different aspects of Terraria, you can now go through the game again, with new challenges. Some players do this with only allowing a certain type of weapon. For example, it could be a boomerang only, or bow only. This can make it harder to get the weapons that will actually help you, making it fun! Another way of playing is to increase the difficulty to master mode, which makes each enemy have more health and do more damage. Some players are really good, they try to beat the game without taking damage during fights!
